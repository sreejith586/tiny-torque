> Status: **draft v2, 2026-10-20**, from `docs/plan/07-collectors-world.md` §9 and §10. Publishes with the first World flag (W03). Copy tone to be aligned with Varun's prototype voice before publishing. Open questions marked ⚖️.

## The short version

Be the collector you'd want to visit. Garages, guestbooks, meetups, rooms, clubs and races are shared spaces — treat them like a friend's garage.

## 1. Respect

- No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, or doxxing (sharing anyone's phone, address, workplace or exact location).
- Blocks work everywhere: a blocked collector can't see your garage, message you, visit, RSVP to your meetups, or race you — and vice versa.
- Hosts can mute, kick or ban in spaces they host. Tiny Torque can override any host.

## 2. Honesty about cars

- No promoting counterfeits, or customs passed off as originals.
- Describe condition and packaging truthfully. Reviews are for completed deals only.

## 3. Marketplace talk

- No selling, price talk or "DM me for price" inside meetups and rooms — share a marketplace listing link instead. Deals happen through listings and offers, and settle between collectors off-platform.

## 4. Brands and names

- Use brand and series names to describe a car, never to imply sponsorship. No brand logos in garage names, decor, signage or meetup titles. ⚖️ Varun: avoid trademarked series names (e.g. "Treasure Hunt") in event-format names.

## 5. Credits fair play

- Credits are yours to spend in the app, not to trade, gift, sell or farm. No bots, multi-accounts, exploits or buying/selling accounts. Exploits are reversed and may cost you the account.

## 6. Racing

- Races are simulated on our servers; results can be replayed from the seed. Tunes and level together move a result by at most 10 %. Prizes are cosmetic — never credits from paid entries. Disputes: use Report on the race; we re-run the seed.

## 7. Hosting meetups and rooms

- Hosts must be Level 2 ("Collector") or above with no upheld report in the last 90 days. Two upheld reports suspend hosting.
- Hosting fees are platform fees. Attending is free.
- Keep the showcase on topic; best-in-show voting is one vote per attendee.

## 8. In-person meetups (listings only)

- Public venues only. 18+ only. No alcohol in the listing. No collecting money through Tiny Torque.
- The listing shows a city and approximate area; share the exact venue only with confirmed attendees.
- Hosts are responsible for local permits, venue permission and local rules. ⚖️ UAE public-gathering/venue compliance wording.
- **Tiny Torque is not the organiser** of any in-person event and may remove any listing. Meet safely: tell someone where you're going, bring a friend, trade in the open.

## 9. Reporting and what happens next

- Report any garage, guestbook message, meetup, room message, race, club or profile from the ⋯ menu.
- First action within 24 hours, resolution within 72 hours, with an appeal path. Serious cases (threats, illegal content) are escalated immediately.
- The audit trail is kept so decisions can be reviewed.

## 10. Minors

Tiny Torque and Collectors World are for adults 18+. Accounts found to belong to minors are closed.
