## 1. Who we are

Tiny Torque ("the Service") is operated by Hypermax FZE (UAE) ("we", "us"). ⚖️ Confirm the contracting entity for Indian users and whether a local grievance address is required under the IT Rules 2021.

## 2. Eligibility — adults only

You must be **18 years or older** to create an account, to buy or use credits, and to take part in Collectors World. By confirming your age at sign-up you declare this is true. We may suspend an account we reasonably believe belongs to a minor. ⚖️ India DPDP Act 2023 treats everyone under 18 as a child; confirm that self-declaration plus the invite gate is sufficient while no under-18 flow exists.

## 3. Your account

One account per person. Keep your sign-in code private. You are responsible for activity on your account. Pilot access is by invitation.

## 4. Your garage and your content

You own the photos and descriptions you upload. You grant us a worldwide, non-exclusive licence to host, display and share them within the Service (including in public garages, meetups and share images you choose to create). Do not upload content you do not have the right to share. Brand and series names are used nominatively for cataloguing; nothing on the Service is sponsored by or affiliated with any toy or car manufacturer.

## 5. Marketplace deals happen off-platform

Listings, offers and chat help collectors find each other. **Payment, shipping and hand-over happen between you and the other collector, outside the Service.** We are not a party to any sale, hold no funds and offer no escrow. ⚖️ Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 (India) — confirm the marketplace-intermediary posture and required disclosures for shop accounts.

## 6. Credits

6.1 **What credits are.** Credits are a prepaid, closed-loop utility credit redeemable only for features inside the Service (listing slots, scan packs, Pro, garage upgrades, items, hosting and entry fees, Spotlight). They have no cash value.
6.2 **What credits are not.** Credits cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, transferred to another user, gifted, tipped, traded, sold, or redeemed with any third party. There is no interest. We never pay credits out as a prize funded by paid entries, and we never sell random-reward items. ⚖️ Confirm this keeps the product a closed-system PPI (RBI) and outside VARA/SCA virtual-asset scope.
6.3 **Purchased credits** never expire. **Earned credits** (from quests, streaks, participation and similar) expire **180 days** after they are granted if unspent; we show the expiry in your history and spend earned credits first.
6.4 **Prices** are shown in your local currency inclusive of applicable tax. ⚖️ India OIDAR GST registration before credit sales to Indian consumers go live; UAE VAT on the Hypermax invoice.
6.5 **Refunds.** Unspent purchased credits may be refunded within 14 days of purchase on request. ⚖️ Confirm the window per country. A refund processed after credits were spent is debited from your balance; a negative balance suspends spending until settled. Items already consumed are not reversed.
6.6 **Chargebacks.** A first chargeback debits the balance; a second may result in revocation of items bought with the disputed credits and suspension.

## 7. Virtual items, levels and progress

Garage levels, decor, themes, tunes, trophies and other virtual items are **licences to use a feature of the Service**, not property. They are personal to your account, cannot be transferred or sold, and may be changed, rebalanced or withdrawn with notice when the Service changes. Prices and progression rules may change over time. Items bought are not refunded when rules change, except as required by law.

## 8. Collectors World conduct

Garages, guestbooks, meetups, rooms, clubs and races are community spaces. The Community Guidelines (v2) apply and form part of these Terms. Hosts may curate and remove content in spaces they host; we may override any host. We may limit, suspend or remove any World feature for any account at any time to protect the community or comply with law.

## 9. Races and events are games of skill and chance with cosmetic prizes

Races are simulated on our servers from your car's catalogue data, condition and your progress, with a random element. Entry fees for bracket events are platform fees. **Prizes are cosmetic items, XP, titles and season points only — never credits, money or goods.** Free-entry community races may award small credit bonuses from our promotional budget. ⚖️ India Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act 2025 — confirm "online social game" classification with these rules; UAE: confirm no GCGRA licence is triggered.

## 10. In-person meetups

Meetup listings are posted by collectors. **We are not the organiser of any in-person event**, do not collect money for them, and do not publish exact addresses publicly. Attend at your own risk; hosts are responsible for venue permission and local rules; attendees must be 18+. Report any problem through the app.

## 11. Acceptable use

No harassment, hate, sexual content, doxxing; no counterfeit or misrepresented items; no price talk inside meetups except via a marketplace link; no brand logos or claims of sponsorship in names and titles; no bots, multi-accounts, earn farming, or exploiting bugs; no collecting payment through the Service for events.

## 12. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or close accounts that breach these Terms. You may delete your account at any time; see the Privacy Policy for what is retained (ledger records are kept for accounting with your identity pseudonymised).

## 13. Changes to these Terms

We will show you the new version at sign-in and record your acceptance. Continued use after acceptance means the new version applies.
